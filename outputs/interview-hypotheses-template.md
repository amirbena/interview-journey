# Interview Hypotheses Template

Markdown presentation template for the Interview Hypotheses output, built from an [Interview Hypothesis record](../schemas/interview-hypothesis.schema.md) per [Framework 08 Required Output](../frameworks/08-interview-hypothesis-framework.md#required-output). All values below are synthetic.

## Disclaimer

> Hypotheses are testable claims about what the interviewer may be trying to validate — they are not facts about the interview.

## Required Sections

1. Top Interview Hypotheses
2. Evidence for Each Hypothesis
3. Confidence
4. Likely Validation Method
5. Preparation Implications
6. Open Questions

## Synthetic Example

### Hypothesis: Resume Verification
**Why likely:** Resume claims sole ownership of a production Kafka redesign; strong resume claims are commonly probed.
**Evidence:** Resume states "Redesigned Kafka consumer pipeline."
**Confidence:** Medium.
**Likely validation method:** Deep-dive follow-up questions on the redesign decision.
**Preparation implication:** Prepare a detailed ownership narrative with trade-offs and metrics.
