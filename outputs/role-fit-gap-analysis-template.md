# Role Fit & Gap Analysis Template

Markdown presentation template for the Role Fit & Gap Analysis output, built from a [Fit & Gap Analysis record](../schemas/fit-gap-analysis.schema.md) per [Framework 04 Required Output](../frameworks/04-role-fit-gap-analysis-framework.md#required-output). All values below are synthetic.

## Disclaimer

> Gaps reflect missing or weak evidence, not proof of missing ability. A "Resume Evidence Gap" means the resume likely under-documents real experience.

## Required Sections

1. Overall Role Fit
2. Top Strengths
3. Strong Matches
4. Partial Matches
5. Critical Gaps
6. High Priority Preparation Topics
7. Resume Evidence Gaps
8. Open Questions
9. Confidence

## Synthetic Example

### Overall Role Fit
Good — strong backend and messaging ownership; one Critical gap in exact-Kafka production depth.

### Strong Matches

| Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Distributed reliability | Owned Kafka-based billing pipeline in production |

### Critical Gaps

| Requirement | Gap Type | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Idempotent payment retries | Experience Gap | Critical |

### High Priority Preparation Topics
1. Idempotency and retry handling 2. Transaction boundaries.
