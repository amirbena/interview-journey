# Role Intelligence Template

Markdown presentation template for the Role Intelligence output, built from a [Role Intelligence record](../schemas/role-intelligence.schema.md) per [`core/output-contracts.md`](../core/output-contracts.md#1-role-intelligence) and [Framework 01 §7](../frameworks/01-role-intelligence-framework.md#7-required-output-schema). All values below are synthetic.

## Disclaimer

> This analysis is inference from the supplied role information, not a confirmed statement from the company. Confidence labels indicate evidence strength, not importance.

## Required Sections (Professional depth)

1. Role Snapshot
2. Source Quality Assessment
3. Business Context
4. Hiring Hypothesis
5. Primary and Secondary Archetypes
6. Seniority Assessment
7. Core Outcomes
8. Technical Expectations
9. Behavioral Expectations
10. Ownership Model
11. Complexity Profile
12. Requirement Priority Table
13. Hiring Signals
14. Likely Interview Focus
15. Candidate Risk Categories
16. Preparation Implications
17. Confidence and Open Questions

Quick and Standard depth use the shorter section sets defined in [Framework 01 §29](../frameworks/01-role-intelligence-framework.md#29-output-templates).

## Synthetic Example (Standard depth)

### Role Snapshot
Senior Backend Engineer at Northbridge Payments, owning revenue-critical payment services on a microservices/Kafka/PostgreSQL stack.

### Hiring Hypothesis
The company is hiring a senior backend engineer to own revenue-critical payment services and improve their reliability as transaction volume grows.

### Archetype and Seniority
Primary: FinTech Backend. Seniority: Senior (score 21/32).

### Core Technical Expectations
Distributed reliability (Critical, 94), End-to-end service ownership (Critical, 91), Data consistency and transactions (Critical, 88).

### Interview Focus
System Design — Very Likely. Distributed Systems — Very Likely. Coding — Likely.

### Preparation Priorities
1. Payment workflow design 2. Idempotency and retry handling 3. Transaction boundaries.

### Confidence and Open Questions
Confidence: Medium overall (JD is detailed; no direct hiring-manager statement yet). Open question: What is the current interview stage?
