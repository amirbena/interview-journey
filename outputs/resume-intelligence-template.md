# Resume Intelligence Template

Markdown presentation template for the Resume Intelligence output, built from a [Resume Intelligence record](../schemas/resume-intelligence.schema.md) per [`core/output-contracts.md`](../core/output-contracts.md#2-resume-intelligence) and [Framework 02 Required Output](../frameworks/02-resume-intelligence-framework.md#required-output). All values below are synthetic.

## Disclaimer

> This analysis reflects what the resume claims and what can reasonably be inferred from it. Unlisted experience is not assumed to exist.

## Required Sections

1. Resume Snapshot
2. Core Technical Strengths
3. Capability Matrix
4. Ownership Profile
5. Production Experience
6. Business Impact Summary
7. Leadership Signals
8. Resume Risks
9. Open Questions
10. Confidence Assessment

## Synthetic Example

### Resume Snapshot
Dana Levi — 7 years backend engineering, most recently owning a Kafka-based billing pipeline at a mid-size fintech.

### Capability Matrix

| Capability | Source Technology | Depth | Ownership |
|---|---|---|---|
| Event-driven architecture | Kafka | Level 3 — Owned in production | Owned |
| Caching | Redis | Level 2 — Worked independently | Implemented |

### Business Impact Summary
Reduced billing pipeline p99 latency by 40% by introducing a Redis caching layer.

### Resume Risks
One role lists only a technology list with no measurable outcome.

### Confidence Assessment
Medium — strong evidence for backend ownership; leadership signals are limited to one mentoring mention.
