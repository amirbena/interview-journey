# Behavioral Story Map Template

Markdown presentation template for the Behavioral Story Map output, built from [Candidate Answer records](../schemas/candidate-answer.schema.md) (`answer_domain: Behavioral`) per [Framework 11 Output](../frameworks/11-behavioral-interview-framework.md#output). All values below are synthetic.

## Disclaimer

> Every story below is drawn from real candidate experience. No story is invented or embellished beyond what the candidate described.

## Required Sections

1. Competency Coverage
2. Best Stories
3. Missing Stories
4. Weak Stories
5. Coaching Feedback
6. Improvement Actions

## Synthetic Example

### Competency Coverage

| Competency | Story | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Kafka consumer redesign | Strong — clear ownership, measurable result |
| Leadership | Mentored 2 junior engineers | Moderate — needs a concrete outcome |
| Conflict Resolution | — | Missing |

### Missing Stories
No strong Conflict Resolution story yet — candidate should identify one real disagreement and its resolution.
