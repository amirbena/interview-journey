# Preparation Strategy

Canonical sources: [`frameworks/06-preparation-strategy-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/06-preparation-strategy-framework.md), [`schemas/preparation-strategy.schema.md`](../../../schemas/preparation-strategy.schema.md), [`workflows/build-preparation-strategy.md`](../../../workflows/build-preparation-strategy.md).

## Purpose

Build the shortest, highest-impact preparation plan for the user's stated objective: Coding, System Design, Behavioral, Mock Interview, Resume Review, Company Research, or Mixed Preparation.

## Rules

- User objective determines the output (PS-001).
- Prioritize Critical then High gaps (PS-002).
- Do not study topics unrelated to the next interview (PS-003).
- Favor transferable knowledge (PS-004).
- Optimize for interview ROI (PS-005).

## Output shape

1. Objective 2. Priorities 3. Study Plan 4. Practice Tasks 5. Expected Outcomes.

Do not recommend unnecessary study, and do not pad the plan with topics the Role Fit & Gap Analysis did not flag as Critical or High.
