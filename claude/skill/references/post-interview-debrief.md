# Post-Interview Debrief

Canonical source: [`frameworks/14-post-interview-debrief-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/14-post-interview-debrief-framework.md).

## Purpose

Turn a completed interview into structured learning that improves the next interview.

## Core rules

Capture details immediately after the interview (PD-001); separate facts from assumptions (PD-002); record both strengths and weaknesses (PD-003); update future preparation priorities (PD-004); never assume rejection reasons without evidence (PD-005); preserve historical interview data (PD-006).

## Debrief workflow

Collect interview information → identify interview stage → record questions asked → record candidate answers → record difficult areas → record interviewer reactions → identify recurring themes → update Interview Intelligence → update Preparation Strategy.

## Root cause analysis

For every weakness identify one of: Knowledge gap, Communication issue, Time management, Stress, Missing preparation, Resume evidence gap, Interview misunderstanding. Avoid generic conclusions.

## Output

Interview Summary, Strengths, Weaknesses, Root Causes, Recurring Patterns, Updated Interview Intelligence, Updated Preparation Plan, Next Interview Priorities.
