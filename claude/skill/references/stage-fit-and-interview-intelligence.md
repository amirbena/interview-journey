# Stage, Fit, and Interview Intelligence

Canonical sources: [`frameworks/03-interview-stage-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/03-interview-stage-framework.md), [`frameworks/04-role-fit-gap-analysis-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/04-role-fit-gap-analysis-framework.md), [`frameworks/05-interview-intelligence-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/05-interview-intelligence-framework.md), [`schemas/interview-stage.schema.md`](../../../schemas/interview-stage.schema.md), [`schemas/fit-gap-analysis.schema.md`](../../../schemas/fit-gap-analysis.schema.md), [`schemas/interview-intelligence.schema.md`](../../../schemas/interview-intelligence.schema.md).

## Interview Stage

Twelve supported stages: Recruiter/HR Screen, Hiring Manager, Technical Coding, Practical Coding/Pair Programming, System Design, Behavioral, Technical Deep Dive, Take-Home Assignment, Final Interview, Executive Interview, Offer Stage, Unknown. Always identify the current stage before generating a preparation plan; known stage overrides generic hiring-process assumptions; optimize for the next interview, not the entire process.

## Role Fit & Gap Analysis

Compare capabilities, not technology names. Match levels: Strong Match, Partial Match, Weak Match, Unknown, Gap. Gap types: Knowledge, Experience, Production, Scale, Architecture, Leadership, Communication, Domain, Resume Evidence Gap. Missing evidence is not proof of missing ability. Prefer transferable capabilities over exact tool matching (e.g., RabbitMQ experience partially satisfies Kafka expectations).

## Interview Intelligence

Continuously enrich preparation from recruiter conversations, hiring-manager comments, previous questions, feedback, take-home assignments, and user observations. New evidence overrides generic assumptions; preserve chronology; never ignore recruiter hints; convert every insight into a preparation action; keep historical context — never overwrite it.
