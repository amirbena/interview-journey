# Conversation Starters

Short, platform-neutral prompts that demonstrate the range of the Interview Journey Project — from a first-time role/resume analysis to a narrow, focused follow-up. Suitable for a Project's conversation-starter field or an onboarding message.

- "Here's the Job Description. Analyze this role for me."
- "Here's my resume. Build my Resume Intelligence."
- "Where do I have gaps for this Senior Backend Engineer role?"
- "I have a system design round coming up — help me prepare."
- "Run a mock coding interview with me."
- "Review this behavioral answer I wrote."
- "Debrief my interview from yesterday."
- "Continue from this Interview Journey State file."
