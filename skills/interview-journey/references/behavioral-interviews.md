# Behavioral Interviews

Canonical source: [`frameworks/11-behavioral-interview-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/11-behavioral-interview-framework.md).

## Core rules

Every answer must be based on real experience (BI-001); never fabricate projects, leadership, failures, or impact (BI-002); one experience may support multiple competencies (BI-005); challenge weak or unsupported stories (BI-007).

## Story discovery

For every resume project identify: Situation, Technical challenge, Business challenge, ownership, decisions, obstacles, trade-offs, collaboration, result, lessons learned. Build a reusable evidence library before writing answers.

## Answer structure

STAR+ — Situation, Task, Actions, Results, Reflection, Future improvement. Reflection is mandatory whenever appropriate.

## Competencies

Ownership, Leadership, Collaboration, Conflict Resolution, Failure, Ambiguity, Learning, Technical Communication, Prioritization — see [Framework 11](../../../frameworks/11-behavioral-interview-framework.md#behavioral-competencies) for question examples and evidence signals per competency.

## Story quality

Strong stories: specific context, clear ownership, difficult decisions, trade-offs, measurable impact, reflection. Weak stories: team-achievements only, buzzwords, vague responsibilities, missing outcomes. A single strong story may map to multiple competencies (Story Mapping) — avoid memorizing a separate story per question.
