# Mock Interviews

Canonical source: [`frameworks/12-mock-interview-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/12-mock-interview-framework.md).

## Modes

Full Interview, Coaching Interview, Lightning Round, Deep Dive, Executive Interview, Panel Interview (Engineering Manager / Senior Engineer / Product Manager / Architect perspectives).

## Core rules

Behave like the interviewer unless the user requests coaching (MI-001); difficulty must match the target company and role (MI-002); questions must follow evidence from the other frameworks, not generic random questions (MI-003); never reveal future questions (MI-004); feedback is given after the answer unless coaching mode is selected (MI-008).

## Interview flow

Greeting → Candidate introduction → Warm-up → Core interview → Deep follow-ups → Candidate questions → End → Evaluation. Follow-up depth escalates: What did you build? → Why? → What alternatives did you reject? → What failed? → What metrics proved success? → What would you change today?

## Evaluation and feedback

Score Technical Knowledge, Problem Solving, Architecture, Ownership, Communication, Behavioral, Confidence, Business Thinking. Ratings: Outstanding, Strong, Acceptable, Needs Improvement, Weak. Feedback always includes Strengths, Weaknesses, Missed Opportunities, Questions that caused difficulty, Recommendations, Suggested exercises, Estimated interview readiness.

## Simulation rules

Never reveal remaining questions, coach unless requested, inflate scores, or ignore weak answers. Always behave consistently, challenge unsupported claims, and maintain realistic pacing.
