# Role and Resume Intelligence

Canonical sources: [`frameworks/01-role-intelligence-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/01-role-intelligence-framework.md), [`frameworks/02-resume-intelligence-framework.md`](../../../frameworks/02-resume-intelligence-framework.md), [`schemas/role-intelligence.schema.md`](../../../schemas/role-intelligence.schema.md), [`schemas/resume-intelligence.schema.md`](../../../schemas/resume-intelligence.schema.md), [`workflows/analyze-role.md`](../../../workflows/analyze-role.md), [`workflows/analyze-resume.md`](../../../workflows/analyze-resume.md).

## Role Intelligence

Convert role information into: Role Snapshot, Business Context, Hiring Hypothesis, Primary/Secondary Archetypes, Seniority Assessment (8 dimensions, 0–4 each), Core Outcomes, Technical Expectations, Behavioral Expectations, Ownership Model, Complexity Profile, Requirement Priority Table (0–100 score: Business Impact 0–25, Core Responsibility Alignment 0–20, Ownership Relevance 0–15, Evidence Strength 0–15, Repetition 0–10, Interview Relevance 0–10, Non-Substitutability 0–5; 90–100 Critical, 75–89 High, 50–74 Medium, 25–49 Low, 0–24 Exclude), Hiring Signals, Likely Interview Focus, Candidate Risk Categories, Preparation Implications, Confidence and Open Questions.

Never score requirements before identifying business context. Never assign more than one primary and two secondary archetypes. A "Lead" title does not automatically imply people management — distinguish technical leadership from management.

## Resume Intelligence

Convert a resume into: Resume Snapshot, Core Technical Strengths, Capability Matrix (technology → capability, depth 0–4, ownership level: Contributed/Implemented/Designed/Owned/Led/Standardized/Mentored), Production Experience, Business Impact Summary (Problem/Action/Result), Leadership Signals, Resume Risks (only when evidence-supported), Open Questions, Confidence Assessment.

Never invent experience. Convert every technology into a capability — never leave a bare tool list. Unknown is better than guessing.
