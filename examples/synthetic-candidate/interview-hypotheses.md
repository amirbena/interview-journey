# Interview Hypotheses — Solstice Cloud System Design Round (Synthetic)

> Fully fictional, produced by [Framework 08](../../frameworks/08-interview-hypothesis-framework.md) for demonstration only.

## Hypothesis: Resume Verification (Ownership)

**Why likely:** Resume claims sole ownership of a measurable, high-impact pipeline redesign — strong claims like this are commonly probed in depth.
**Supporting evidence:** Resume states "Reduced invoice-generation latency from ~6 hours to under 5 minutes."
**Confidence:** High.
**Likely validation method:** Deep-dive follow-up questions on specific design decisions and what Maya personally decided versus what the team decided.
**Preparation implication:** Prepare a detailed, decision-by-decision account, not just the headline metric.
**Disproving evidence:** If the interviewer never returns to resume projects and stays purely hypothetical.

## Hypothesis: Production Readiness

**Why likely:** The role is revenue-critical billing infrastructure; System Design rounds for this archetype typically test failure handling and operational judgment.
**Supporting evidence:** Role Intelligence flags "Production ownership risk" as a candidate risk category the company is likely probing for.
**Confidence:** Medium.
**Likely validation method:** A scenario question about a dependency failure or regional outage.
**Preparation implication:** Rehearse failure-handling and cross-region reasoning even without direct resume evidence.
**Disproving evidence:** If the round stays purely at the requirements/API-design level without any failure scenario.
