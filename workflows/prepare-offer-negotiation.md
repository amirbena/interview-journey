# Workflow: Prepare for an Offer or Negotiation

Use this focused workflow when [Framework 15](../frameworks/15-interview-journey-intelligence-framework.md) identifies offer or compensation-negotiation intent. Apply the canonical contract in [`core/offer-negotiation-preparation.md`](../core/offer-negotiation-preparation.md); this workflow defines execution order, not a second methodology.

## Steps

1. Confirm the objective: offer call, expectations question, employer-range response, offer evaluation, or active negotiation.
2. Reuse relevant role, stage, market, employer-range, candidate-preference, competing-process, and total-compensation context already available.
3. Ask one focused question only if the missing answer materially changes the preparation; otherwise state an assumption and proceed.
4. Gather or normalize attributable market evidence when market figures materially affect the answer. Preserve figure/range, population/context, source, retrieval date, and freshness/fit for every material row.
5. Separate user/employer facts, public evidence, general guidance, and assumptions. Preserve contradictions and population mismatches.
6. Build the Target range, Preferred outcome, and Fallback. Treat the fallback as the candidate's decision boundary; if it is unknown, provide a provisional rule rather than inventing one.
7. Compare total compensation and material terms where relevant.
8. Prepare natural principles plus candidate-ready answers for the relevant compensation questions.
9. Return the canonical output at Quick, Standard, or Professional depth.

## Completion gate

Apply the validation checklist in [`core/offer-negotiation-preparation.md`](../core/offer-negotiation-preparation.md#validation-checklist) before responding.
