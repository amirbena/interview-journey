# Post Interview Debrief Framework

**Document ID:** 14
**Version:** 1.0
**Depends on:** Documents 01–13

> **Canonical framework.** See [`core/orchestration-policy.md`](../core/orchestration-policy.md).

---

# Purpose

Turn every completed interview into structured learning.

The goal is to continuously improve future interview performance using evidence from previous interviews.

---

# Core Rules

## PD-001
Capture details immediately after the interview.

## PD-002
Separate facts from assumptions.

## PD-003
Record both strengths and weaknesses.

## PD-004
Update future preparation priorities.

## PD-005
Never assume rejection reasons without evidence.

## PD-006
Preserve historical interview data.

---

# Debrief Workflow

1. Collect interview information.
2. Identify interview stage.
3. Record questions asked.
4. Record candidate answers.
5. Record difficult areas.
6. Record interviewer reactions.
7. Identify recurring themes.
8. Update Interview Intelligence.
9. Update Preparation Strategy.

---

# Information to Capture

## Logistics
Company, Role, Date, Interview stage, Interviewers.

## Technical Topics
Coding, System Design, Resume Deep Dive, Architecture, Domain Knowledge.

## Behavioral Topics
Leadership, Ownership, Failure, Collaboration, Motivation.

## Candidate Reflection
Strong answers, Weak answers, Surprises, Missed opportunities, Confidence level.

---

# Root Cause Analysis

For every weakness identify: Knowledge gap, Communication issue, Time management, Stress, Missing preparation, Resume evidence gap, Interview misunderstanding.

Avoid generic conclusions.

---

# Trend Detection

Track recurring patterns across interviews. Examples: System Design repeatedly weak; Strong coding performance; Resume explanations improving; Leadership stories lacking evidence.

Focus on trends rather than isolated events.

---

# Action Planning

For every important finding define: Priority, Improvement action, Practice recommendation, Expected outcome.

---

# Interview Intelligence Updates

Update when appropriate: Company preferences, Interview style, Frequently asked questions, Unexpected topics, Hiring signals.

---

# Required Output

1. Interview Summary
2. Strengths
3. Weaknesses
4. Root Causes
5. Recurring Patterns
6. Updated Interview Intelligence
7. Updated Preparation Plan
8. Next Interview Priorities

See [`schemas/interview-debrief.schema.md`](../schemas/interview-debrief.schema.md), [`outputs/post-interview-debrief-template.md`](../outputs/post-interview-debrief-template.md), and [`workflows/run-post-interview-debrief.md`](../workflows/run-post-interview-debrief.md).

---

# Validation Checklist

- Facts separated from assumptions.
- Root causes identified.
- Improvement plan created.
- Interview Intelligence updated.
- Lessons are reusable.

---

# Final Principle

Every interview should improve the probability of succeeding in the next one.

No interview is wasted if structured learning is captured.
