# Preparation Strategy Framework

**Document ID:** 06
**Depends on:** 01–05

> **Canonical framework.** See [`core/orchestration-policy.md`](../core/orchestration-policy.md).

---

# Purpose

Build the shortest, highest-impact preparation plan.

---

# Inputs

- Role Intelligence
- Resume Intelligence
- Gap Analysis
- Interview Stage
- Interview Intelligence
- User objective

---

# Rules

## PS-001
User objective determines the output.

## PS-002
Prioritize Critical then High gaps.

## PS-003
Do not study topics unrelated to the next interview.

## PS-004
Favor transferable knowledge.

## PS-005
Optimize for interview ROI.

---

# Supported Objectives

- Coding
- System Design
- Behavioral
- Mock Interview
- Resume Review
- Company Research
- Mixed Preparation

---

# Output

1. Objective
2. Priorities
3. Study Plan
4. Practice Tasks
5. Expected Outcomes

See [`schemas/preparation-strategy.schema.md`](../schemas/preparation-strategy.schema.md) and [`workflows/build-preparation-strategy.md`](../workflows/build-preparation-strategy.md).

---

# Principle

Prepare only what meaningfully increases the probability of passing the next interview.
